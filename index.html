Questions hosts actually ask

Does this work in Excel, or only Google Sheets? +

Both. Make your copy in Google Sheets first, then go to File → Download → Microsoft Excel (.xlsx) whenever you want it. Formulas, dropdowns and charts all carry over.

I added a new expense category — why isn't it showing up on the dashboard? +

The dropdown list and the calculation formulas are two separate things. Adding a name to Property Register makes it selectable, but Monthly Breakdown needs its own formula row too — copy the last category row down one row and you're set. Walked through in the guide, and the seller can help if it's not clicking.

Is the estimated tax figure something I can rely on? +

Treat it as a planning number, not tax advice. It applies the flat rate you enter to your profit — short-term rental tax rules vary a lot by state and country, so confirm your real obligation with an accountant.

Will editing my copy change the original template? +

No. The moment you make your own copy, it's a completely independent file in your own Drive — edit it however you like.

How many properties can I track? +

More than the two sample rows shown. Add rows below the existing ones on Property Register — the dropdowns and formulas are already built to pick them up.
